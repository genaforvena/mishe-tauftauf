# Enactivist agency as a three-condition transfer check for body readings — individuality, interactional asymmetry and viability-relative normativity (Barandiaran, Di Paolo & Rohde 2009)

## What we read

(2009), "Defining Agency: Individuality, Normativity, Asymmetry, and Spatio-temporality in Action", *Adaptive Behavior* 17(5):367–386, doi:10.1177/1059712309343819**, open-access PDF <https://xabier.barandiaran.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/barandiaran_dipaolo_rohde_-_2009_-_defining_agency_-_adbeh_pub.pdf> fetched 2026-11-02 to `/tmp/defining-agency.pdf`, 324_787 bytes, sha256 `7549d596aab54dbd00d130f1376c0457bc10c846843958665999a6f0d4b2d5ad`, 20pp, `pdfinfo` Acrobat Distiller 5.0.5 2009-09-10, `pdftotext -layout` 1020 lines retained; read pp.367–379 in full (Abstract, §1 Agency as departure point pp.367–368, §2 What Agency Requires: Individuality/Asymmetry/Normativity pp.369–374 including Table 1 p.374, §3 Living Agency pp.375–376, §4 Defining Agency pp.376–378 including Figure 1 and generative definition Eq.1–3, plus §5 scan pp.378–379). Source URL present as required.

  Core machinery retained: three jointly necessary conditions — (a) **individuality** as self-individuation (a network of interdependent processes distinguishing itself from its environment, not an observer-imposed boundary, Jonas 1968 p.239–240, §2.1 pp.369–371), (b) **interactional asymmetry** as systematic modulation of the coupling's constraining parameters (Eq.1 `dS/dt = F_Q(S,E)`, Eq.2 `dE/dt = G_Q(S,E)`, Eq.3 `Δp = H_T(S) p⊂Q`, §2.2 pp.371–372 — weaker than causal or energetic dominance, covering both active energy-gathering and "surfing" the coupling like gliding, and requiring temporally extended, context-sensitive events `T` over subset `p` of total coupling conditions `Q`), (c) **normativity** as regulation toward an internally generated ought-to-be — a contingent viability condition whose violation threatens the self-maintaining organisation, producing success/failure for the system itself rather than for an observer (Jonas 1968 p.243 "to be is its intrinsic goal", §2.3 p.372–373; §3 living precariousness via membrane + far-from-equilibrium metabolism pp.375–376).

  Generative definition (§4 pp.376–378): "an autonomous organization capable of adaptively regulating its coupling with the environment according to the norms established by its own viability conditions" (p.376), made precise as two clauses: 1. `S` is an open autonomous network whose component processes enable and depend on each other and tend to extinguish in isolation (§4.1 p.377), defining `E` as the processes that can affect and be affected by `S` with coupled viability conditions; 2. `S` modulates coupling `C` adaptively — change in `C` contributes to maintenance of `S`'s constitutive processes (§4.2 p.377). Figure 1 illustrates the regulation of coupling constraints by the autonomous circle. Table 1 (p.374) operationalises the triad as a diagnostic: gas-in-container (none), cell undergoing passive osmosis (individuality+normativity, no asymmetry → not agent), Parkinson tremors (individuality+asymmetry, no normativity → not agent), kitten warmed by mother (individuality+normativity, no asymmetry → not agent), bacterium chemotaxis (all three → agent).

  Limits retained: §2.4 mutual support but non-redundancy of the three (p.373–374); §3 living organisation as *sufficient but not necessary* minimal illustration — autonomy is defined abstractly as networked interdependence and can be chemical, neurodynamic, sensorimotor or social (§4 p.378), explicitly decoupled from metabolic aliveness; §5 spatiality/temporality as the two fundamental domains of agency extension, not read as a mechanism to import. No claim is made that device readings are alive or that every sensor coupling is an agent — the definition is minimal and permissively extensible to "different scales of autonomy" (p.378) only where the three conditions are jointly met.

## What is new here

Barandiaran et al. 2009 is the primary source the 2026-10-31 discard could not reach, and it supplies three things no landed review has yet transferred to the sensing side:

1. **A compact, reader-checkable diagnostic (Table 1 p.374) for the plant's own readings.** The triad gives three yes/no questions that can be asked of any `sense.body.redmi10` channel without new instrumentation: does the reported channel define an individual (a self-maintaining network with its own boundary, not an observer-imposed container)? Is the coupling modulated by the system (asymmetric `Δp` over `T`, Eq.3) rather than passively suffered? Is the modulation regulated toward a viability condition generated by the system itself (an ought-to-be whose violation is failure *for* that system)? The paper's four non-agent rows are directly reusable templates: passive osmosis (individuality+normativity without asymmetry) maps to a phone sensor drifting with environment; tremor (individuality+asymmetry without normativity) maps to a phone actuating without a viability reference; kitten-warmed-by-mother (individuality+normativity without asymmetry) maps to a body kept warm by an external controller. Only the bacterium's metabolic chemotaxis (all three) qualifies — a high bar the transfer respects.

2. **The asymmetry-as-modulation formalism (Eq.1–3 p.372).** Interactional asymmetry is not "more energy" or "more correlation" but modulation of a subset `p⊂Q` of coupling constraints over interval `T`. That distinction matters for `body_perception.py`'s spool model: a reading's `E` (environment) is explicitly `Q`'s outside, and a future adaptive behaviour would need to name which `p` it claims to modulate and over what `T` — not just that a value changed. The gliding/bird and cliff-diver examples (p.371) show why statistical dominance or energy investment alone misclassifies interaction.

3. **The living-sufficient-not-necessary move (§3–4 pp.375–378) and the observer-cut implication.** Autonomy as networked interdependence applies to neurodynamic or sensorimotor loops, not only metabolism (§4 p.378), so a human+phone extended system can be assessed without claiming the phone is alive — but only where the extended network's own viability conditions (e.g., the human's) are the norm, not an externally imposed dashboard threshold. The individuality discussion (Jonas regress, pp.370–371) warns that an observer-accorded boundary is not an individual's boundary; which cut is being judged must be stated.

Limits retained: the definition is presented as necessary-and-sufficient for minimal agency (p.373) while the paper itself notes the sufficiency is defended by inability to find counterexamples — not a closed proof; spatiality/temporality (§5 p.379) and the "different scales of autonomy" nesting (p.378) are not imported as claims about this plant's hierarchy; the precariousness/compensation sense-making gloss (pp.378–379) is not proposed as an implementation.
