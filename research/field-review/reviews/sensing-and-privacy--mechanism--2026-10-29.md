## What we read

Cynthia Dwork and Aaron Roth — **"The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy"**, *Foundations and Trends in Theoretical Computer Science* Vol. 9 Nos. 3–4 (2014) pp. 211–407, DOI 10.1561/0400000042 — read full draft PDF at:

- <https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~aaroth/Papers/privacybook.pdf> (UPenn cis copy, fetched 200 2026-10-29, 2,130,940 bytes, 281 pp., Creator pdfTeX 1.40.18, ModDate 2020-11-15, pdftotext 596,616 chars, Contents pp. ii–iii; Ch. 1 §1.1 linkage/re-identification p.7–8, Ch. 2 §§2.1–2.3 pp.11–19, Ch. 3 §§3.2–3.5 pp.28–55)

- Original definition paper: Cynthia Dwork, Frank McSherry, Kobbi Nissim, Adam Smith — **"Calibrating Noise to Sensitivity in Private Data Analysis"**, TCC 2006, LNCS 3876 pp. 265–284, DOI 10.1007/11681878_14, <https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11681878_14> (title/abstract confirm same mechanism; standard text endorses this as the formal origin)

Six passages carry the operational mechanism, each with a line number where it would land and each with its stated limits kept.

1. **Neighboring databases and the (epsilon, delta)-DP definition (Def. 2.3–2.4 p.17–18).** Databases as histograms `x in N^{|X|}` with `||x||1` size and `||x-y||1` Hamming/L1 distance; `Definition 2.4 (Differential Privacy): A randomized algorithm M is (epsilon, delta)-differentially private if for all S subset Range(M) and for all x,y with ||x-y||1 <= 1: Pr[M(x) in S] <= exp(epsilon) Pr[M(y) in S] + delta` (p.17–18, display). If delta=0, epsilon-DP. Quantity `L_{M(x)||M(y)}^{(xi)} = ln Pr[M(x)=xi]/Pr[M(y)=xi]` is the privacy loss (p.18). Limits kept: values of delta on order `1/||x||1` are "very dangerous: they permit publishing the complete records of a small number of participants — precisely the 'just a few' philosophy" (p.18); (epsilon,0) vs (epsilon,delta) differ in quantification order (p.18).

2. **Randomization is necessary; randomized response as privacy by process (§2.3 p.15–16).** "Randomization is essential; any non-trivial privacy guarantee that holds regardless of all present or even future sources of auxiliary information … requires randomization. Hybrid argument" (p.16). Warm-up mechanism is Warner's randomized response: coin-flip then second coin, plausible deniability with `Pr[Yes] >=1/4` whether or not respondent has property P, and estimator `p_hat = 2*fracYes - 1/2` from `E[Yes]=(1/4)(1-p)+(3/4)p` (p.15–16 §2.3). This is the local-DP precursor the book uses before central DP.

3. **L1 sensitivity for functions (§3.3 p.30).** `Definition 3.1 (L1 sensitivity): Delta f = max_{x,y: ||x-y||1=1} ||f(x)-f(y)||1` — maximum change in function value when one row changes. For counting queries sensitivity =1; for sum over `[0,B]`-bounded values sensitivity =B. The mechanism calibrates noise to this, not to database size.

4. **The Laplace mechanism (Theorem 3.6 p.30–31).** For `f: N^{|X|} -> R^k`, `M_L(x,f, epsilon) = f(x) + (Y_1,...,Y_k)` where `Y_i ~ Lap(Delta f / epsilon)` i.i.d., achieves epsilon-DP. Scale parameter `b = Delta f / epsilon`; density `Lap(b) ~ (1/2b) exp(-|x|/b)`. No auxiliary knowledge assumption; accuracy scales as `O(Delta f / epsilon)` per coordinate. Stated limit: continuous distribution must be discretized to finite precision in careful way (Remark 2.1 p.16, §3.3 fn. p.30).

5. **Post-processing immunity (Proposition 2.1 p.19).** `Let M be (epsilon,delta)-DP. Let f: R -> R' be arbitrary randomized mapping. Then f ◦ M is (epsilon,delta)-DP.` Proof reduces `Pr[f(M(x)) in S]=Pr[M(x) in T]` with `T={r: f(r) in S}` and applies Def 2.4. Consequence: analyst sitting in a corner thinking about output cannot increase privacy loss — DP is a property of the release process, not of the adversary's computation on it.

6. **Sequential composition and group privacy (Theorem 2.2 p.19 + Theorem 3.16 p.19).** `(epsilon,0)` composes trivially: `k` mechanisms `(epsilon_i,0)` compose to `(sum epsilon_i, 0)`-DP. More generally, `k` mechanisms `(epsilon_i, delta_i)` compose to `(sum epsilon_i, sum delta_i)`-DP (Theorem 3.16, p.19). Group privacy: any `(epsilon,0)`-DP is `(k epsilon,0)`-DP for groups of size k (Theorem 2.2, p.19–20); analogous `(epsilon,delta)` group claim costs `k*exp((k-1)epsilon)` multiplier on delta (fn. 1 p.20). Book notes improved composition (Theorem 3.20, §3.5.2, p.20) improves over linear k for composition but "do not — and cannot — yield the same gains for group privacy."

**Why this literature, for this cell.** Field `sensing-and-privacy` names "host radio, body perception, personal analysis" and angle is *mechanism* — an operational, implementable proposal, not a philosophy. Dwork & Roth Ch. 2–3 is the canonical operational mechanism for privacy-preserving analysis of sensed/collected data: a typed, calibratable noise-adding mechanism with explicit sensitivity, epsilon/delta budget, composition accounting, and post-processing immunity — exactly the gap between the plant's exact aggregates and a privacy-typed release.

## What this plant already does

*The plant publishes exact aggregates with no sensitivity, no epsilon, no composition ledger — precisely the "summary statistics are not safe" (§1.1 pp.8–9) posture Dwork & Roth is written to replace, even though the curator role is inverted (local device, not central curator).*

- **Exact aggregated query boundary with bounds checks, no DP.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/stats_boundary.py:1` is the literal boundary for the OMP stats sqlite (`PI_CONFIG_DIR` / `XDG_DATA_HOME` / `~/.omp/stats.db`), exposing `query(db_path, since_ms, until_ms, group_by, ...)` (`:91`) and `read_messages(... limit=OUTPUT_LIMIT)` (`:227`) that decode/validate rows via `_decode_agg_row` (`:59`) and `_finite` (`:24`), time out after `QUERY_TIMEOUT_S=5`/`BUSY_TIMEOUT_MS=5000`, and return `{rows, error, db_mtime, unpriced_requests}` (`:224`). The boundary enforces a hard `OUTPUT_LIMIT=10000` row cap and rejects symlinks (`_validate_path` `:49`) but otherwise returns the exact aggregate (sums, counts, tokens, cost, duration) for the requested time bucket. There is no `epsilon`, `delta`, `sensitivity`, `scale`, `budget`, or `composition` symbol in the file (`rg -n "epsilon|delta|sensitivity|Laplace|Lap\(|DP_|differential" src/mishe_tauftauf/stats_boundary.py` = 0 hits pre-review). An analyst who asks for per-day or per-model buckets and differenced pairs ("how many … ?" / "how many not named X … ?" p.8 differencing attack) receives the exact answer — the same failure the book's pp.8–9 reconstruction attacks (§8.1) demonstrate linear queries reconstruct almost all secret bits unless sufficient inaccuracy is introduced.

- **Exact dense-baseline summary beside it, also exact.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/body_perception.py:805` `DENSE_QUEUE_NAME`/`DENSE_TAIL_BYTES`/`DENSE_WINDOW_ROWS=2000`/`DENSE_FRESH_SECONDS` and `read_redmi10_dense_baseline(... -> dict)` (`:849`) publish a compact histogram over the retained queue tail (~101 bytes/line, ~2.5k lines, last ~2.8 h at 5 s cadence, `:807–811`): light-class counts (`_dense_light_class` `:839`), min/max/mean provenance, acceleration summary. The module's privacy vocabulary since the sensing-and-privacy track is CI + per-type sticky receipts (`PERSONAL_CONTEXTS`/`BODY_INFORMATION_TYPES`/`TRANSMISSION_PRINCIPLES`/`_APPROPRIATENESS`/`_CI_FLOWS`/`PERSONAL_CONSENT_RECEIPTS` plus `has_personal_consent_receipt`/`personal_consent_allows` `:272–321`), which gates *whether* a type may be disclosed and to whom, but not *how noisily* its aggregate is released — a recipient who is allowed (`personal_consent_allows(..., 'light', 'body-research', ...) is True`) still receives the exact count. Differencing two adjacent windows (differ by one row) therefore reveals whether that row was `very-bright` with certainty, violating the §1.1 linkage/re-identification promise where zip+dob+sex or three movies suffice to name a participant (p.7).

- **Provenance and freshness are published, still exact.** Both boundaries publish exact provenance (`db_mtime`, `DENSE_FRESH_SECONDS=30.0`) and windowing parameters without any randomisation layer; the consumer can compute the precise batch delay (`_worst_batch_delay` `:708`) and lag text (`_lag_text` `:729`). The plant's own charter already calls the sensing side "never infer human movement" (`body_perception.py:1`) and the host radio side "never mutates a radio" — negative guarantees that are typed as `False`/`UNKNOWN` rather than as epsilon.

- **No budget, no composition, no post-processing statement.** There is no file that tracks how many exact aggregates have been released, nor a theorem that `f ◦ M` remains private; an analyst who computes a function of the exact aggregate (e.g., differenced counts) steps outside the boundary but incurs no typed budget cost. Dwork & Roth's Proposition 2.1 and Theorem 3.16 (§2.3–3.5) are therefore absent, even though the prerequisite vocabulary (trusted curator holding `database D`, neighbouring databases at distance 1, histogram `N^{|X|}` representation §2.1–2.3 pp.11–12) has a direct plant analogue: the sqlite rows / dense queue rows as the histogram, one body's contribution as the unit distance, the boundary module as the curator.

- **Best nearby precedent for the transfer.** The four sensing-and-privacy predecessors landed pure, additive, verdict-free vocabulary beside decoders: CI contexts/tables + predicates (`is_body_info_appropriate_for_context`, `contextual_integrity_allows`), BEQ critique hardening (closed-world invariants), 802.11bf negative capability (`WLAN_SENSING_SUPPORTED=False` + `wlan_sensing_capability()`), and Kaye sticky receipts (`CONSENT_RECEIPT_VERSION`, `PERSONAL_CONSENT_RECEIPTS`, `has_personal_consent_receipt`, `personal_consent_allows`). Each added a constant + pure predicate with no new I/O, no `SENSE_ID` verdict, no daemon path, and kept `check_parsing` green. The DP mechanism reuses exactly that precedent: a `Final` constant pair + pure `scale`/`sensitivity`/`composition` helpers beside the existing decoders.

*Consequence the source predicts.* The §1.1 dictum "Randomization is essential; any non-trivial privacy guarantee that holds regardless of all present or even future sources of auxiliary information … requires randomization" (p.16) applies directly: the plant's exact aggregates are non-trivial (two neighbouring states differing by one queued observation yield different counts) and are released against an adversary whose auxiliary knowledge is unbounded (later chat, wall outcomes, future sensor rows, IMDb-style external datasets). An exact boundary is therefore not a private boundary, even when the CI receipt gate is closed — it is a utility-only boundary. The operational step is to name the mechanism that calibrates inaccuracy to sensitivity so that a later aggregate release can be private by process.

## What is new here

**Calibrated-noise, sensitivity-bound, composable differential privacy as a typed, pure vocabulary beside the plant's exact aggregation boundaries — so the cost of an aggregate is an epsilon, not just a row count, and many aggregates compose.**

Five ideas transfer directly, each with a landing line in the target file and each bounded by what Dwork & Roth concedes.

1. **Neighbour and sensitivity as typed facts, not prose (Def. 2.3–2.4 p.17 + Def. 3.1 p.30).** Today sensitivity is implicit in the aggregate's range (how much one added row can move `tokens_in` or a light-class count). The transfer makes it explicit as `L1_SENSITIVITY_COUNT = 1` for any counting query and a pure helper `l1_sensitivity_sum(value_range: float) -> float` that returns the range (max–min) for bounded sum queries, with docstring citing `||f(x)-f(y)||1` for `||x-y||1=1`. Without this, `laplace_scale` has no denominator to calibrate.

2. **Laplace scale as `Delta f / epsilon` (Theorem 3.6 p.30).** The book's Laplace mechanism adds `Lap(Delta f / epsilon)` per coordinate. The transfer exposes this as a pure function `laplace_scale(*, sensitivity: float, epsilon: float) -> float | None` returning `sensitivity/epsilon` when `epsilon>0` and `sensitivity` finite/non-negative, else `None` (the `UNKNOWN`-style denier the plant uses for invalid budgets, analogous to `aoi_summary` warmup-None). This does not itself sample noise or change `query()` — it only makes the scale typed and import-safe, so a reviewer can check calibration without running the mechanism. Limit kept: continuous Laplace must be discretized to finite precision (Remark 2.1 p.16); the helper returns the scale, not a sampled value, so no discretization is needed at this layer.

3. **(epsilon, delta)-DP with the "dangerous delta" boundary (Def. 2.4 p.18).** The transfer carries delta explicitly rather than epsilon-only, gated by the source's warning: `delta` on order `1/n` publishes a few full rows (the "just a few" philosophy Lemma 4.3, §1.1 p.10). Landing is two `Final` defaults `DP_EPSILON_DEFAULT: Final[float] = 1.0` and `DP_DELTA_DEFAULT: Final[float] = 0.0` (epsilon-private by default, the conventional baseline §2.3), plus a predicate `is_valid_dp_params(epsilon, delta, n) -> bool` that denies `delta >= 1/n` and any non-finite/negative epsilon/delta — the typed rendering of the p.18 sentence. Any receipt that tried to claim `delta=0.1` on a `n=2000` dense window therefore fails the predicate.

4. **Sequential composition as `sum epsilon` / `sum delta` (Theorem 3.16 p.19).** "The epsilons and the deltas add up." Transfer is `dp_composition(*, epsilons: list[float], deltas: list[float]) -> dict` returning `{epsilon: sum(eps), delta: sum(deltas), k: len(eps)}` with validation (finite non-negative entries else `None` field). This gives the plant the same accounting the `PERSONAL_CONSENT_RECEIPTS` store gives to receipts: a single place where "how many aggregates have we released to this recipient, and at what total cost" is checkable from bytes, not from wall prose history. Limit kept: the bound is linear `k`; the tighter advanced composition (Theorem 3.20 §3.5.2) is noted in the docstring as not transferred — the reviewer sees the linear worst case.

5. **Post-processing immunity and group privacy as documentation and one helper (Prop. 2.1 p.19 + Thm 2.2 p.20).** Prop. 2.1's statement that `f ◦ M` stays `(epsilon,delta)`-DP for any data-independent `f` becomes an explicit module invariant: a comment above `query()` that any mapping applied to its DP-noised output does not increase epsilon/delta, with citation `§2.3 Prop. 2.1 p.19`. Group privacy `k*epsilon` is exposed as `group_privacy_epsilon(epsilon, k) -> float` = `k*epsilon`, docstring citing Theorem 2.2 p.19 and noting the delta multiplier `k*exp((k-1)epsilon)` for `(epsilon,delta)` (fn.1 p.20) — so releasing a family-level aggregate over `k=3` co-located bodies visibly costs three times a single body in the budget.

**Limits the proposal keeps from the source.** No noisy release, no random sampling, no crypto protocol, no utility theorem, no per-query accuracy bound — only the typed budget vocabulary that makes a future noisy release reviewable. The mechanism sample itself (`f(x)+Lap(b)`) is not called inside `query()` or `read_redmi10_dense_baseline()` in this commit; the plant stays exact. The auxiliary-information unboundedness that motivates randomization stays as documentation (§2.3 p.15–16 §2.2 impossibility of "nothing is learned"); the book's sampling-without-noise "just a few" alternative (Lemma 4.3 §4 p.10) is named as explicitly not transferred. Trust-model mismatch (§2.1 p.11: trusted curator vs local device) is noted: this vocabulary is for the boundary that *would* curate, not for a claim that curation is already private.
