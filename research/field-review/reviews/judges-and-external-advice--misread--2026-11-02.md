## What is new here

- The misread is not the control prose — it is importing Zheng's headline numbers and remedies as if they validate or fix a pointwise yes/no judge. The paper's "over 80%" (abstract) is 85% S2 GPT-4 vs humans _after_ excluding ties/inconsistencies in pairwise MT-bench; Fig.2 shows that agreement is ~70% when model win-rate disparity is small, rising to near 100% only for large gaps. Reading 85% as blanket validity overstates close comparisons — exactly the regime where this plant's `desired-state-met` must discriminate `reading=42` vs `41`.
- Table 2 consistency 65% (GPT-4 default) means more than a third of pairwise calls flip when answers are swapped; the paper's conservative mitigation is "call twice and declare tie if inconsistent" — a pairwise construction that has no literal analogue for a single-document pointwise judge. The docs' order permutation (swap `TOP PAIN`/`EVIDENCE` inside one document) is the pointwise analogue; copying "swap positions and tie" verbatim would be a category error, and §3.4's few-shot mitigation (77.5% at 4× cost, "may not imply high accuracy") is not imported either.
- Verbosity Table 3: even GPT-4 fails 8.7% on a list doubled with paraphrased duplicates _without new information_, while Claude/GPT-3.5 fail 91.3%. Identical answers tie correctly, duplicated-information answers do not. The plant's length-padding check uses neutral filler and warns filler sentiment can flip a verdict — that warning is required by the paper's design (rephrased list kept information constant). One confident-claim case or one padded pair is a probe, not the paper's bias rate; measuring verbosity needs a length-matched set, as the paper's 23-answer attack does.
- Self-enhancement (Fig.3b): GPT-4 +10% self win rate, Claude +25%, but also favor other models and GPT-3.5 shows no self-favor; paper says "Due to limited data and small differences, our study cannot determine whether the models exhibit a self-enhancement bias. Conducting a controlled study is challenging because we cannot easily rephrase a response to fit the style of another model without changing the quality." A discard that treats self-preference as proven misreads the paper's own caution.
- Limited reasoning Table 4: math failure 70% default → 15% reference-guided. The remedy that works is showing the judge its own independently generated reference answer — not chain-of-thought alone (30%). For this plant, the analogue is not a generic CoT prompt; it is the grounded SYSTEM ZERO output itself as reference (exit/stdout), which the document already supplies. An operator who asks a pointwise judge to re-derive the check result without that evidence is reproducing the paper's failure mode.
- Only ordering and qualitative design transfer to the pointwise categorical 1/0 adapter; Zheng's specific percentages, the swap-twice mitigation, and the reference-generation step are pairwise/open-ended QA constructs. The bound is the same as Tripathi's Likert 1..7 caveat: direction transfers, numbers and mechanism do not.
