# ECE is the wrong scoreboard — rank order flips when you fix how you bin

## What we read

**Jeremy Nixon, Mike Dusenberry, Ghassen Jerfel, Timothy Nguyen, Jeremiah Liu, Linchuan Zhang, Dustin Tran — "Measuring Calibration in Deep Learning", arXiv:1904.01685v2 [cs.LG] 7 Aug 2020 (v1 2 Apr 2019)** — read full primary source at:

- <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01685> (arXiv abstract, v2 7 Aug 2020)
- <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.01685.pdf> (PDF 595_460 bytes fetched 200 2026-10-30 at /tmp/nixon19.pdf, 14 pp., sha256 7c3faa8397a4c6f544a883038236e13bca54d9b03fb125e66b666e8eece79617, header `Measuring Calibration in Deep Learning`)

Nixon et al. is the direct critique of Guo et al. (2017) "On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks" that this plant's field-review `model-judgment--misread` just land­ed. Four passages carry the critique this plant would reproduce if it adopted Guo's ECE blindly. Page numbers are arXiv v2 pagination; quotations via `pdftotext`.

1. **ECE has numerous flaws and cannot evaluate recalibration success — framing (§1 Abstract + p.1–2).** "Expected calibration error, the most popular metric, has numerous flaws which we outline, and there is no clear empirical understanding of how its choices affect conclusions in practice" (Abstract). "In this paper, we show that ECE has numerous pathologies, and that recent calibration methods, which have been shown to successfully recalibrate models according to ECE, cannot be properly evaluated via ECE" (p.1, col.2). The paper studies MNIST/Fashion-MNIST/CIFAR-10/100/ImageNet and varies five axes (§4): all-vs-max probabilities, thresholding, class conditionality, number of bins, adaptive vs even bins, L1 vs L2 norm — and then optimizes temperature scaling directly for each metric to measure sensitivity.

2. **Four pathologies of static even-bin ECE (§3 pp.3–4, Figs.1/3/4).** §3.1 "Not Computing Calibration Across All Predictions": ECE "is computed using only the predicted class's probability, which implies the metric does not assess how accurate a model is with respect to the K−1 other probabilities … ECE becomes a worse approximation as relevant predictions not included … become common (Figure 1)." Fig.1 Left shows per-class ACE varies sharply across classes on CIFAR-100 ResNet-110. §3.2 "Fixed Calibration Ranges": with sharpness "large leftward skew … only a few bins contribute most to ECE — typically one or two as bin sizes are 10-20." §3.3 bias-variance bin tradeoff. §3.4 "Pathologies in Static Binning Schemes": "you can get near 0 calibration error due to overconfident and underconfident predictions overlapping in the same bin … cancellation effects" — Fig.4 (p.12 code) constructs 450 predictions at 0.52 all incorrect + 550 at 0.58 all correct in one even bin → ECE ≈ 0.003 despite "extreme miscalibration."

3. **General Calibration Error and the five remediable axes (§4 pp.4–5, SCE/ACE/GCE definitions).** GCE captures variants by (i) class conditionality (§4.1 — bin per class then average, not lumping), (ii) adaptivity (§4.2 ACE — `ACE = (1/KR) Σ_r Σ_k |acc(r,k)−conf(r,k)|`, bins hold equal count by sorted quantiles `bN/R`th index), (iii) maximum-prob only vs all probabilities (§4.3 SCE — `SCE = (1/K) Σ_k Σ_b nbk/N |acc(b,k)−conf(b,k)|`, bins per class, zero iff calibrated in limit), (iv) norm (§4.4 `|·|` vs `√Σ(·)²`), (v) thresholding (§4.5 `ε=0.01/0.001` — discards infinitesimal softmax tails that waste bins). Label codes `0–31` (p.12 Appx.C) enumerate the cross product `binning×maxProbs×classConditional×threshold×norm` (0 even/true/true/0.0/l1 … 31 adaptive/false/false/0.01/l2).

4. **Empirical consequences — rank order is unstable and Guo's recipe is outlier-brittle (§5 pp.5–8, Tables 1–5).** Tables 1–2: ECE underestimates class-conditional error by 3× on CIFAR-10 and 5× on ImageNet for the same histogram-binning recalibration. Table 3 (Imagenet ECE after optimizing for each property): even-scheme optimization gives mean ECE 0.0322 vs adaptive 0.0480; L1 0.0535 vs L2 0.0267 — ECE "is dominated by similarity to itself, rather than capturing a more general notion." Table 4: mean rank correlation across bin counts 10–50 is 0.59 for adaptive vs 0.37 for even, 0.55 class-conditional vs 0.40 unconditional — adaptivity stabilizes ordering. Table 5 (32 metrics × 8 recalibrations on CIFAR-10): "dramatic inconsistency between calibration ordering … varying properties can lead to different conclusions about which model has best calibration." Recommendations (Abstract + §6): use class-conditional, adaptive bins, L2 norm, and GCE over ECE; open-sourced library.

**Limits the source states and this review keeps.** The authors concede (§6 Criticisms): adaptive bins "can create excessively large ranges if there is very sparse output … may be better to have higher variance estimate … dropping far predictions" — granularity vs interpretability tradeoff and "increase within-range variance." Results are for vision classification (MNIST/ImageNet) with softmax logits and held-out validation tuning; thresholds `ε=0.01/0.001` and bin counts are reporting choices, not laws. GCE still needs digest-checked held-out pairs; no threshold or norm transfers as a tuned number.

**Why this source, for this cell.** `model-judgment--critique` asks for the known failure mode we would reproduce. The plant's `model-judgment` field just adopted Guo's ECE/MCE/reliability (misread review a074…) and Wang's `log p → softmax(z/T)` (recent review) — exactly the "ECE as scoreboard" practice Nixon proves misranks methods. Without the critique, the plant's next `τ` tuning would be judged by the one metric designed to flatter its own assumptions.

Those are exactly ECE's §3.1–3.4 flaws: the two helpers cannot see secondary `P(no)/P(unknown)` miscalibration, will cancel opposite errors inside one even bin (Fig.4), and will move ranking when `M` changes.

- **completions_judge.py — categorical adapter with Wang invert trick and a single ECE.** Module doc "Categorical yes/no map to 1/0 … not calibrated" (`:4`); `model-judgment--recent` adds `invert_softmax_logits`/`softmax_with_temperature`/`expected_calibration_error(bins=10)`/`calibration_summary` — again one even-bin L1 ECE over the predicted confidence.

- **docs/decision-models.md + docs/completions-judge.md — evaluation plan names calibration as disclaimer.** `decision-models.md:48` "incomplete calibration," `:41` "benchmark calibration does not transfer" — calibration read as vendor trait, not as binned measurement whose rank order Nixon shows to be unstable (Tables 3/5). No class-conditional, adaptive, thresholded, or L2 reporting; no pathology detector.

- **What the misread costs if the critique is ignored.** Guo's temperature scaling (§3.2 vs Nixon Fig.1 Right) minimizes a different loss surface than ECE: "temperature that minimizes calibration error is not same as temperature that minimizes cross entropy" (Nixon Fig.1 caption). Optimizing `T` for the plant's even-bin L1 ECE will select a `τ_q` that looks best on ECE but is 3–5× worse on class-conditional ECE (Tables 1–2) and that flips ordering under adaptive bins (Table 3 0.0322→0.0480). A future `τ=1.7` claim would then read as "calibrated" while remaining miscalibrated on every `P(no)` the smoke suite never measures.

## What is new here

**Nixon's critique is a test harness for the plant's calibration numbers: make every ECE publication state the five choices it made, and ship the three measurements that catch its bias — class-conditional, adaptive, and L2 — plus the one-line cancellation litmus that proves a near-zero score can be maximally wrong.**

1. **Rank-order instability is the finding, not a footnote — report GCE coverage, not ECE alone (Nixon §5, Tables 3–5).** Mean ECE after optimizing for L1 is 0.0535 vs L2 0.0267 on ImageNet; even vs adaptive flips 0.0322↔0.0480; 32 GCE variants disagree on which of 8 recalibrations is best. Consequence: a single `ece=0.013` without `M / even-vs-adaptive / max-vs-all / class-conditional / threshold / L1-vs-L2` stated is unreproducible. The plant's `ECE_DEFINITION` must record the 5-tuple `binning,maxProbs,classConditional,threshold,norm` (Appx.C order codes `0–31`) beside every reported number — same determinism as Guo's `M=15` but now complete.

2. **Class conditionality and all-probabilities — ECE hides the error you care about (Nixon §3.1/§4.1/§4.3).** `SCE` bins per class; `ACE` averages per `(range,class)`. On CIFAR-10 standard histogram binning, `ECE=0.0070` but `class-conditional ECE=0.0189`; on ImageNet `0.0147→0.1561` — same predictions, opposite conclusion (Tables 1–2). For this plant with `K ∈ {2 (yes/no), 3 (yes/no/unknown)}`, ECE over `P̂_yes` alone cannot detect that `P(no)=0.42` is systematically wrong. The guard is to report `sce`/`classConditional` alongside `ece` whenever `K>2`, and to note the omitted `K−1` mass.

3. **Adaptive bins + L2 + thresholding are the recommended defaults because they stabilize what even/L1 obscures (Nixon Abstract + §5.3/§4.2/§4.5).** Adaptive equal-count bins raise mean rank correlation 0.3677→0.5927 when varying bins 10→50; per-class adaptivity 0.4056→0.5546; all-probabilities 0.3565→0.6037 (p.6). L2 improves optimization and consistency. Very tiny `p≈0` for many classes wash out the score (Fig.3 Top Left, §A.1) — thresholding at `ε=0.01/0.001` discards the noise tail. Practical guard: ship an `adaptive_calibration_error` that sorts confidences and cuts at quantiles `⌊bN/R⌋`, and an `sce`/`ace` that threshold below `ε`.

4. **Static-bin cancellation is the one-line litmus — embed Fig.4 so a 0.003 cannot pass as calibrated (Nixon §3.4 p.12).** 450 at 0.52 incorrect + 550 at 0.58 correct in one `[0.5,0.6]` bin → `acc=0.55, conf≈0.553, |acc−conf|≈0.003` while every prediction is wrong on confidence. Any even-bin checker that reports near-zero without the adaptive/class check replays this exact cancellation. The plant's acceptance suite should include this 1000-point synthetic as a regression: even ECE must be ~0, adaptive must be ≫0.

**Limits the review keeps from Nixon.** Only the *measurement axes and stability caveats* transfer, not a dataset- or model-specific ECE/τ number; no Imagenet CIFAR threshold `ε` is imported as a constant — thresholds and bin counts stay caller-stated. Adaptivity's sparse-range caveat (can create excessively large ranges) is kept. The plant's `unknown` for incomplete evidence stays the detector for missing pairs; the new helpers only add the second clause Nixon distinguishes: *which* `ECE` you computed.
