# Differential articulation, not mutual constitution: what Vila et al. (2025) add to reading the plant's couplings

## What we read

**Pablo Vila & Edward Avery-Natale — "Assemblages and Identitarian Articulations", *Subjectivity*, Vol. 32, pp. 192–220, published online 8 October 2025, DOI 10.1007/s41286-025-00220-6 (Open Access CC BY 4.0)** — read full primary source at:

- <https://doi.org/10.1057/s41286-025-00220-6> (Springer landing)
- <https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1057/s41286-025-00220-6.pdf> (PDF 849_050 bytes fetched 200 2026-11-02 at /tmp/assemblage_identitarian.pdf, 29 pp., sha256 `d7aaaa3c7bc22931dbe57b909a0af312ee60e82805657e8dbefe447076828f3a`, Creator Springer/Acrobat Distiller 10.1.8, Pages 29, Subject "Subjectivity, https://doi.org/10.1057/s41286-025-00220-6")

Vila & Avery-Natale is the recent re-reading of Deleuze & Guattari for this cell; the 1980/1987 ontology (*A Thousand Plateaus*, *Anti-Oedipus*) is read only through their synthesis and citations. Pagination is the journal PDF pagination (192–220); quotations via `pdftotext` of the fetched PDF.

1. **Assemblage as productive desire, not context (pp. 194–195, § Introduction).** Following Deleuze & Guattari (1987) via Buchanan (2020 p.56) and Müller & Schurr (2016 p.224): "Desire is primary; it is desire that selects materials and gives them the properties that they have in the assemblage" (p.194 n.2); "assemblage indicates design, layout, organization, arrangement, and relations" from French *agencement* (p.195), and "An assemblage is not defined by its components; they are defined, rather, by what they produce" (Buchanan 2020 p.47 quoted p.195). Assemblages "are not a distinct form but 'an ongoing process of agencement through which form emerges and may endure'" (Anderson et al. 2012 p.174 quoted p.195). The English "assemblage" reifies what Massumi's "arrangement" keeps purposeful (p.195 n.4).

2. **Identitarian articulation and differential articulation vs "mutual constitution" (pp. 192–193 Abstract, pp. 198–204 §§ Identitarian articulations / Mutual constitution or differential articulation?).** "Identifications" (race, class, gender, familial, religious) are "active processes continuously reconstituted through interactions (with objects, technologies, discourses, and bodies) in assemblages" (Abstract p.192); "Identitarian articulations represent the unique entanglements of these identifications within different assemblages … Assemblages are not static but ongoing processes shaped by desires and interactions among their components (people, objects, technologies, discourses, and emotions)" (Abstract). Crucially: "the different identifications … do not necessarily carry the same weight" (p.199); "their affective capacities are unequal and their weights may vary in different assemblages or different instantiations of the same assemblage, leading to differential articulations, a term that we prefer over the customary 'mutual constitution'" (p.199). Differential means "with what level of intensity" each capacity affects the articulation (p.202) — not every element co-informs evenly, and which capacities fire "is defined by the particular characteristics of the assemblage at stake, including relations of power" (p.199).

3. **Capacities vs properties, intensities, and the virtual (pp. 196–198).** Following but updating DeLanda (2006): "capacities and properties are not entirely separate categories but are infinite cycles of interconnected flows, feeding back and forth" (p.197); "some of the capacities … modify the properties … usually … much longer than the modification of capacities" (p.197, with Sub-Saharan melanin example n.8). Intensity is "speed or energy … some of its potentials and its capacities actualized to greater or lesser effect depending upon the desirous needs of the assemblage" (p.194 n.3). The virtual is "a pre-actualized state … 'Differentiation is the movement of a virtuality actualizing itself' (Deleuze 2002 p.40)" (p.197); "what is actualized from the virtual is like a particle … it was entirely possible that something else would have irrupted … the virtual will always be much grander than the actual" (p.198). The BWO "precedes the organi-ized bodies" as "fields of intensities that will actualize as … identitarian articulations" (p.198).

4. **Intra-action, tendential combinations, and the critique of gaps/points (pp. 199–202).** Via Barad (2007 p.ix): "to be entangled is not simply to be intertwined … but to lack an independent, self-contained existence"; "relations come first and those things that are related second" (p.201). Via Hall (1980 pp.30–31): "tendential combinations … preferred combinations, sedimented and solidified by real historical development" — "historically specific, rather than specified a priori … 'laws of tendency' which can be counter-manded" (p.200, with Weheliye 2014 p.49 gloss). Consequence: "there are no gaps, but there are also no points, only implications and potentialities" (p.202) — gaps between identifications are not missing data but virtual traces (p.201 n.14).

**Limits the source states and this review keeps.** The paper is sociology of identifications (race/class examples via Lareau 2011 pp.202–205, Jim Crow bus p.204–205); no filesystem, pane, or sensor analogue was measured. DeLanda's book (2006) and Deleuze texts are cited second-hand; Massumi/Barad/Hall framings are interventions, not theorems with thresholds. Differential articulation has no numeric intensity scale — it is a qualitative insistence that weight varies by assemblage and moment. The virtual/BWO is explicitly "dis-organ-ized" (p.197) and not inferrable from a single actual (p.198) — the analogy to quantum fields (p.198) is pedagogical. The tension with Barad (relations precede relata vs classic assemblage emergence in parallel, Hein 2016 p.132 n.12) is noted as unresolved.

**Why this source, for this cell.** `deleuze-guattari--recent` asks for a recent (2023–2026) result in the assemblage/rhizome/machinic area, read as primary source, applied to "the plant's own couplings, and what it declares about them" (`fields.json` plant_part). Prior `deleuze-guattari--recent` (2026-10-30) used Hung Sep 2025 DAO-as-rhizome (six rhizome principles, map vs tracing, rupture) via law journal; that review landed at docs/mesh.md. Vila & Avery-Natale Oct 2025 is the newest available peer-reviewed assemblage article retrievable as OA PDF (verified 29 pp. 2026-11-02) and it treats couplings through a *different* D&G vocabulary — differential weight, capacities/properties, virtual/actual, intra-action/tendential trace — not covered by Hung and not embodied in `docs/wall-coordination.md` as written.

## What this plant already does

*The plant declares its couplings, but it declares them as if each carried the same weight in every wake — the rhetoric of mutual constitution while the tape shows differential weight.*

- **docs/mesh.md — declares couplings as a mesh with knots, not a flat network (mesh.md:9–32).** Since Hung's review was absorbed, mesh.md now names the assemblage explicitly: "The plant's couplings are not abstract. They are walls … one `main` branch, a wake … a push gate" (mesh.md:11–15), "Knots are real: the supervisor wakes … `seed yield` serializes … genome pushes `main`" (mesh.md:24–27), and the four diagnostic questions (rupture, hub-free entry, heterogeneity, detachable map vs traced path, mesh.md:46–55 with Berkowitz & Bor 2026 six principles). Mesh.md also absorbed Ovezik et al. 2025 concentration warning (mesh.md:34–44): shares can be summarised as entropy/Gini/Nakamoto/HHI with window/clustering/threshold as the measurement. What mesh.md does *not* yet do is name unequal weight: it stages knots as persistent facts, not as varying intensities that differentially articulate in a particular wake.

- **docs/wall-coordination.md — declares the detailed coupling (head read 2026-11-02, ~80 lines).** Exposes wall write mechanics (`wall write --owner ROLE --file NOTES` attribution, audit `wall-write-audit.jsonl`, peer-copy refusal including superseded digests, `wall show` never-copy peer header), tape reading, lock around settlement, branch/CI gate. The coordination narrative is "Minds choose work … FEED on findings … addressed suggestion starts coordination; it does not finish the work" (wall-coordination.md:6–14) and operator-view/health coverage for leases. Each coupling is described with its rule, but not with its *capacity weight in that assemblage instance* — e.g., in wake 203091 the supervisor wake and `seed yield` lock had high intensity, while a peer DM had low intensity, yet the doc text treats them as coequal channels.

- **What a sweep found.** `research/field-review/field_review.py next` for this cell prior to this review reported `assemblage/rhizome/machinic` hits only via declaration fields and the Hung-derived mesh passage — no file names `differential articulation`, `capacity` in DeLanda's sense, `virtual`, `intra-action`, `tendential`, or `intensity` as Vila uses them. No docs file distinguishes capacities (what a mind *can do* this wake, changeable per encounter) from properties (what the file *is*, e.g., wall persistence, slower to change). The plant's virtual — coordination potential before a wake is issued — is implied by "wall + tape + branch" but never named, so a reader can infer the virtual's size from one actual landing (exactly what p.198 warns against: "it is never the case that what is actualized can tell us what the totality of the virtual might be").

- **What the gap costs if the critique is ignored.** Without differential articulation, every coupling declaration reads as mutual constitution: walls, DMs, wakes, locks, pushes appear to co-inform evenly. Vila's Lareau demonstration shows the cost: Lareau (2011 p.133) concluded "race did not appear to shape … child rearing" because class was treated as primary, while Alexander's mother *did* differentially articulate race intensely when her son risked being "the only black kid" (p.203) — the intensity varied by instantiation of the educational assemblage. The plant analogue: a coupling that matters intensely in one wake (the lock during contended settlement, the bus-seat layout in Stacey's transportation sub-assemblage p.204–205) recedes in another (quiet wake with no settlement race). Declaring couplings without naming that variance invites the same misreading Lareau made — declaring race/class (or wall/lock) neutral on average while missing the high-intensity instantiations where one capacity dominates and where history (sedimented tendential combinations, Hall 1980 via p.200) has already weighted the combination.

## What is new here

**Vila et al.'s 2025 differential articulation: treat a coupling declaration not as coequal "mutual constitution" but as unequal, intensively varying articulation where each capacity's weight differs by assemblage instance, where capacities (fast, per-encounter "I can", Merleau-Ponty 2012 p.139 via p.197 n.6) and properties (slow, sedimented) feedback cyclically, and where relations (intra-action) precede the relata — so the assemblage produces its versions of walls/tape/locks, not the other way around. The transferable audit is to name intensity variance and tendential sedimentation, not a new identity category.**

1. **Differential weight as the declaration test — "differential articulation" over "mutual constitution" (Abstract p.192, p.199, p.202).** The question is not "do wall, DM, wake, lock combine?" but "with what level of intensity at this moment in this assemblage does each capacity fire, and which capacities are trace/virtual rather than actual?" (p.202). Vila shows the method on Lareau: Alexander's mother vs Stacey's mother articulate race/class with different intensities in different *instantiations* of the same educational assemblage (pp.202–205), and the bus driver's cruelty (p.204) makes the bus seat layout — an object — carry high intensity via historical sedimentation. For wall-coordination.md the actionable carry is to state explicitly that wall/tape/lock/branch/Git articulate *differentially* — any coupling can be present as trace without firing — and to mark where history has sedimented a preferred combination (Hall's "tendential combinations", p.200) such as supervisor→wake→wall→yield→push.

2. **Capacities vs properties and the virtual's excess (pp.197–198, DeLanda 2006 via p.197).** Capacities are "practices people are willing and able to perform (or avoid)" (p.196) — the "I can" before "I think that" (Merleau-Ponty 2012 p.139 n.7) — and they "change much faster … everyday interactions eventually … show that some of those capacities are not exercisable" (p.197), while properties sediment slowly via feedback (skin/melanine example n.8). The virtual "will always be much grander than the actual" and "what is actualized" cannot reveal the virtual's totality (p.198 quantum-field analogy). Applied: a mind's capacity to write a wall is fast and per-wake; the wall file's property (its persisted text, its digest in the audit) is slow and sedimented. A single `git log --oneline` actual does not reveal the virtual coordination potential that could have irrupted differently had the lock or CI raced differently — exactly the warning mesh.md now needs beside its single-concentration-number warning.

3. **Intra-action before identities, and sedimented power as tendential (pp.199–201, Barad 2007 p.ix, Hall 1980 p.31).** "It is not that you first get identifications that encounter each other … then combine … Instead … the assemblage itself produces a specific version of race, class … (and a particular version of objects, technologies …) at different moments" (p.201). Applied without importing race/class as plant identities: the assemblage of wake 203441 produced *its version* of "wall", "DM", "lock", "push" for that interval — the wall that was writable, the DM that was routable, the lock that was contended — and history weighted the preferred combinations ("preferred articulations insert historically sedimented power imbalances", Weheliye 2014 p.49 via p.200). That reframes "genome pushes main" not as genome's property but as an intra-action product that *also* produces genome's capacity in that wake.

4. **No gaps, no points — and no new score (pp.201–202).** "There is no gap … there is nothing 'in-between' … each identification is already part of the others, without gaps … there are no gaps, but there are also no points, only implications and potentialities" (p.202) — gaps are virtual traces (p.201 n.14), not absent data. The transfer is therefore not a "differential articulation score" or gate; like Hung's rhizome diagnostic, it is qualitative. Vila's paper offers no metric or threshold (see Limits above), and the BWO as virtual (pp.197–198) is not a dashboard number.

**Limits the review keeps from Vila.** Only the differential/capacities/virtual/intra-action/tendential vocabulary moves; no racial, gender, familial, or religious identification, no Lareau child-rearing, no bus/Jim Crow fact is imported as a plant property. The sociology base (US healthcare, education, transportation assemblages 2024–2025) and the unresolved Barad-vs-classic-assemblage tension (Hein 2016 p.132 n.12) are cited as framing, not as demonstrated traits. No intensity threshold is transferred; variance is declared, not scored.
