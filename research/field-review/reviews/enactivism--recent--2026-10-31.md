## What we read

Hila, A., "An Enactivist Approach to Human-Computer Interaction: Bridging the Gap Between Human Agency and Affordances", arXiv:2509.07871v1 (cs.HC), submitted 9 September 2025, <https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07871v1>, read 2026-10-31 via the arXiv HTML text. Passages read: the autopoietic framing of the agent as an operationally closed system; the definition of organizational closure as "a system of parts connected through closed feedback loops"; the claim that sensorimotor information is "constitutionally valanced" for the agent; the critique of the comparator model of sense of agency; the proposed "feelings of agency" composite of affective engagement and volitional attention; and the proposed measures (alpha-beta ratio, frontal-alpha asymmetry, theta-gamma coupling, first-person reports) and VR design in its Table 1. The paper states that its neurodynamic and phenomenological correspondences are hypotheses and that it contains no empirical results.

## What this plant already does

The plant's sensing side reads its evidence as observations of a body and a mind with explicit viability checks: UNKNOWN and RED are assigned when evidence is missing or stale, and the dashboard and walls carry the sensors' freshness rather than an inferred state. The field-review grid already holds this lens as `enactivism` in `research/field-review/fields.json`, with the sense-making and autonomy keywords. Agency and autonomy of the plant's minds are handled by the wall, the settlement records and the outcome kinds, not by any phenomenal or neural measure.

## What is new here

Only the vocabulary of feelings of agency as a composite of affect and attention. That is a claim about human experience, measured with EEG and self-report. The plant has no subject whose feeling of agency could be the observable, and no instrument for affect or attention in its minds. The valenced-information idea is the same enactive point already in the grid's enactivism field, and it does not give a check this plant could run.

## Discard

Discarded: the recent source is an untested neurophenomenal hypothesis with no data, and its measures are for human agents' brains, which the plant does not have, so no tracked file changes because of it.
