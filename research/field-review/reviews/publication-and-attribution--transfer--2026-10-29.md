## What we read

Walter Benjamin — **"The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction"** (1935, first published 1936; this reading via the Harry Zohn translation from *Illuminations*, 1968, as hosted at):

- <https://www.marxists.org/reference/subject/philosophy/works/ge/benjamin.htm> (HTML, fetched 200 2026-10-29, 59,270 bytes, 19 numbered sections I–XIX; marxists.org ETag "e786-4fe07c731d2c0", Last-Modified Sat, 12 Jul 2014)

Four passages carry the transferable mechanism, each with the plant-level analogue it replaces.

1. **Presence, authenticity and the outside of reproducibility (II).** "Even the most perfect reproduction of a work of art is lacking in one element: its presence in time and space, its unique existence at the place where it happens to be. This unique existence of the work of art determined the history to which it was subject throughout the time of its existence." And immediately: "The presence of the original is the prerequisite to the concept of authenticity. Chemical analyses of the patina of a bronze can help to establish this, as does the proof that a given manuscript of the Middle Ages stems from an archive of the fifteenth century. The whole sphere of authenticity is outside technical — and, of course, not only technical — reproducibility." The source mechanism: authenticity is not a thicker hash; it is the testimony of duration and ownership that a reproduction cannot carry.

2. **Aura as the element that withers, and what replaces it (II–III).** "One might subsume the eliminated element in the term 'aura' and go on to say: that which withers in the age of mechanical reproduction is the aura of the work of art. This is a symptomatic process whose significance points beyond the realm of art." "The technique of reproduction detaches the reproduced object from the domain of tradition. By making many reproductions it substitutes a plurality of copies for a unique existence. And in permitting the reproduction to meet the beholder or listener in his own particular situation, it reactivates the object reproduced." The source mechanism: plurality itself is the withering; aura is "the unique phenomenon of a distance, however close it may be" — not a bug to fix with a better copy.

3. **Cult value vs exhibition value as poles, and the qualitative shift (VI–VII).** "Works of art are received and valued on different planes. Two polar types stand out; with one, the accent is on the cult value; with the other, on the exhibition value of the work. Artistic production begins with ceremonial objects destined to serve in a cult. One may assume that what mattered was their existence, not their being on view." "With the different methods of technical reproduction of a work of art, its fitness for exhibition increased to such an extent that the quantitative shift between its two poles turned into a qualitative transformation of its nature." The source mechanism: cult and exhibition are not a slider; accumulated fitness for exhibition reverses the work's function.

4. **Emancipation from ritual to politics, and the senseless authentic print (IV, VIII).** "For the first time in world history, mechanical reproduction emancipates the work of art from its parasitical dependence on ritual. To an ever greater degree the work of art reproduced becomes the work of art designed for reproducibility. From a photographic negative, for example, one can make any number of prints; to ask for the 'authentic' print makes no sense. But the instant the criterion of authenticity ceases to be applicable to artistic production, the total function of art is reversed. Instead of being based on ritual, it begins to be based on another practice — politics." The source mechanism: the new basis is exhibition/politics (who may publish, who is seen), not restored cult; the "authentic print" question is the boundary check.

**Limits the source states, kept here.** Benjamin concerns mechanically reproducible art (woodcut, lithography, photography, film), not every text; he offers a historical-material diagnosis, not a preservation manual; his politics is 1930s aesthetics, not a general theory of provenance; and his "distance however close" aura is not a metric. The transfer does not claim photography maps mechanically to git; it transfers the structure — plurality withers presence, exhibition displaces cult, politics replaces ritual — onto the commit/DEV reproduction the plant already operates.

**Why this literature, for this cell.** Field `publication-and-attribution` is "the reader book, the DEV blog, commits, CI and pushes" and angle is *transfer* — a cross-domain transfer onto one of this plant's parts. Benjamin 1935 is the canonical account of what changes when a work becomes reproducible at scale: aura withers, tradition detaches, cult yields to exhibition, authenticity becomes unaskable. The plant reproduces Mesh work and its own commits across three surfaces (git chain, wall digest, DEV prose) and already types hashes as provenance — but has no typed distinction between cult and exhibition or between hash-verified copy and recovered presence. The mechanism is exactly the plant's own reproduction.

## What is new here

**Benjamin reframes the plant's reproduction from "how to prove this copy is authentic" to "this is a mechanical reproduction whose aura has withered, whose function is exhibition/politics, and whose authentic-print question must be refused" — and that reframing, not a thicker hash, is the honest-publication boundary.**

1. **Authenticity outside reproducibility as a typed refusal, not a longer digest.** Benjamin's "whole sphere of authenticity is outside technical reproducibility" is not about choosing sha256 vs sha384. The wall-outcome `hex64` and commit `hex40` are hard bindings (`c2pa.hash.data` per recent) that prove bytes were not altered; they do not prove "presence in time and space." A DEV article quoting Mesh review `888faeb` with a correct URL and hash still lacks the Mesh work's "substantive duration" and "testimony to the history which it has experienced" (II). The transfer makes `authentic-print-makes-sense` a pure `False` for any reproduction — so a hash-checked copy cannot be promoted to `Authentic` without stating the missing presence.

2. **Withering as an additive exhibition fact on the candidate, not a defect to fix.** "The technique of reproduction detaches the reproduced object from the domain of tradition." The plant's three disconnected provenance mechanisms (git chain, wall digest, DEV prose) are already three detachments; adding a typed manifest (recent) re-attaches a hash, not a tradition. The transfer names the detachment: a pushed candidate `head:main` and a published DEV article are plural copies meeting the beholder in his situation, and their plurality is the withering, not their lack of hash. A follow-on dock or DEV front matter can cite `mechanical-reproduction` without claiming restored aura.

3. **Cult vs exhibition as the candidate's venue value, not a disclosure level.** C2PA Ch.16.3 `minimal/standard/full` is creator-controlled disclosure; Benjamin's cult/exhibition is a historical function change where "the work of art reproduced becomes the work of art designed for reproducibility." The plant's DEV articles from `4942462` (factory) and `4942558` (rhizome) are exhibition-value objects by design — they exist to be seen on DEV, indexed, commented upon — yet their docs frame them with cult-value language (preserve Mesh aura via attribution). The transfer maps venue `dev`/`commit`/`wall-outcome` → `exhibition` and documented ritual originals → `cult`, so the honest voice is typed: Mishe agents publish as current exhibitors in a political sense, with Mesh cited as ingredient, not as re-hosted presence.

4. **The authentic-print test as the plant's publish-time guard.** "From a photographic negative ... to ask for the 'authentic' print makes no sense." The plant's publish-time check should be: asking which of two bit-identical checkouts of `e1b92f5` is the authentic one makes no sense; asking whether the DEV article `4942558` is the authentic Mesh article makes no sense — it is a reproduction. What does make sense is to judge exhibition fitness and political basis (who was authorized to publish, under what charter, with what ingredients), not to claim recovered aura. The recent vocabulary makes `Valid` vs `Trusted` checkable; Benjamin makes `Authentic vs Reproduction` unaskable — both limits stay visible.

**Limits the proposal keeps from the source.** Benjamin does not provide a hash algorithm, a trust list, or a disclosure level; he does not discuss git, markdown, or the plant's `delivery.py`. The transfer keeps his historical-material limits and does not claim photography/film maps mechanically to commits. It does not propose to restore aura, to stop reproducing, or to make every DEV post a political manifesto — it only makes the withering and the cult→exhibition shift a typed, reviewable fact beside the candidate that is being reproduced. No new wall/chat dialect, no feed I/O, no site-home access.
