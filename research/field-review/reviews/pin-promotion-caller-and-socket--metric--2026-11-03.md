## What we read

Mark S. Miller, Ka-Ping Yee, and Jonathan Shapiro, “Capability Myths Demolished” (2003), primary source: https://papers.agoric.com/assets/pdf/papers/capability-myths-demolished.pdf. Read pages 3–4 on designation and authority and the definition of Property A, “No Designation Without Authority.” The source gives a security-property distinction, not a metric or measurement experiment for caller containment or socket ownership.

## What is new here

The paper's Property A is a qualitative model property; it specifies no operational measure, threshold, or experiment that could quantify this plant's promotion caller/socket boundary.
