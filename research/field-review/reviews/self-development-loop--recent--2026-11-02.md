The paper posits that superhuman agents require superhuman feedback, but human-preference reward models are bottlenecked by human performance and frozen during training. It studies Self-Rewarding LMs where the LM itself, via LLM-as-a-Judge prompting, provides its own rewards during Iterative DPO training — instruction following and reward-modeling ability co-improve.

Four passages carry the change this plant has not made (distinct from the prior `self-development-loop--recent` DGM 2025 reading on empirical code-archive evolution):

1. **The self-rewarding hypothesis — frozen external judge vs. self-judge that learns (§1 Abstract, §1 Introduction, §2).** Classical RLHF/RLAIF trains a separate frozen reward model from human preferences; the paper states this is bottlenecked by human level and cannot improve during LLM training. Self-Rewarding replaces it: the same LM generates answers and, prompted as LLM-as-a-Judge, scores them (Figure 2 prompt with 5-point rubric plus chain-of-thought justification, `§2.3 Figure 2`, `§2.1 EFT Seed Data` p.5). The judge ability is trained jointly with instruction following, not as a frozen external scorer. Limit kept: the claim is about *training signal* scaling, not that any self-judgement is automatically correct — §1 notes the separate model "cannot then learn to improve during LLM training" and §2 requires judged seed data that agrees with human rankings before acceptance.

2. **EFT + IFT joint initialization as the seed that makes self-judgement possible (§2.1 Initialization, §2.1 Seed LLM-as-a-Judge instruction following data, §3.1.1, §3.2.1).** The model is initialized from Llama 2 70B with two instruction-tuning corpora: 3,200 Open Assistant IFT examples (highest human rank 0) and a constructed Evaluation Fine-Tuning (EFT) dataset derived from the same ranked responses placed in the Figure 2 judge format, where SFT-baseline generations of justifications/scores are kept only when score ranking agrees with human ranking, rebalanced away from the common score 4 (p.6). Result: `M1` (EFT+IFT) performs on par with `SFT baseline` (IFT alone) on head-to-head instruction following (30.5% wins vs 30.9%), meaning EFT training does not degrade instruction following (§3.2.1 p.8). Limit kept: EFT seed quality depends on human-ranked agreement filtering — without that filter the judge would reinforce noise.

3. **Iterative self-alignment algorithm — AIFT(M_t) produces M_{t+1} via DPO on self-scored preferences (§2.2 Self-Instruction Creation, §2.3 AI Feedback Training, §2.4 Overall Self-Alignment Algorithm, §3.1.3).** Each iteration: generate new prompts with fixed Llama 2-Chat 70B + 8-shot Self-Instruct (6 IFT + 2 self-generated, T=0.6 p=0.9, ROUGE-L/keyword/length filtered), generate candidate responses with current `M_t`, score them with same `M_t` as judge (3 samples averaged), build preference pairs (AIFT), train `M_{t+1}` from `M_t` via DPO. Sequence: `M1 = EFT+IFT`, `M2 = DPO_AIFT(M1)`, `M3 = DPO_AIFT(M2)` (p.5–6, list `M1/M2/M3`). Iterations added 3,964 pairs for `M2` and 6,942 for `M3` (§3.1.3 p.7). Limit kept: §2.4 explicitly calls the procedure "Iterative DPO" (Xu et al. 2023 Pairwise Cringe) but with self-judge rather than external fixed reward; the paper fixes the prompt-generation model and sampling params — not every token is self-generated.

4. **Dual improvement metric — instruction following and reward modeling co-evolve, measured as pairwise accuracy/exact match/Spearman/Kendall tau (§3.1.2 Evaluation Metrics, §3.2 Results, Table 1 Figure 3).** Reward modeling is evaluated on Open Assistant held-out pairs (avg 2.85 responses/instruction) as pairwise accuracy (how often pair order agrees with human), exact match (total order), Spearman, Kendall tau, and fraction of 5/5 scores that are human-top-ranked (p.7). Instruction following is GPT-4 head-to-head win rate (both orders, disagree→tie), AlpacaEval 2.0 win rate vs GPT-4 Turbo (805 prompts), MT-Bench (GPT-4 grade/10), plus 9 NLP benchmarks to check no regression (p.6–7). Result: `M1≈SFT`, `M2` beats `M1` (55.5% vs 30% range) and `SFT` (49.2% vs 14.5%), `M3` beats `M2` (47.7% vs 12.5%) — same pattern on AlpacaEval 2.0 (9.94%→15.38%→20.44%, beating Claude 2 17.19%, Gemini Pro 16.85%, GPT-4 0613 15.76%) — and EFT-augmented reward modeling improves then further improves with self-training (§3.2.2), with ablation showing LLM-as-a-Judge prompt is critical. NLP benchmarks do not regress (§3.2.1 last paragraph). Limit kept: evaluation leans on GPT-4-as-judge and Claude 2 validation (§3.1.3 early stopping via Claude 2 pairwise on 253 validation examples) — the paper trades one external judge for another at evaluation, not claiming judge-free ground truth.

## What this plant already does

*The plant observes but never self-rewards — its judges are frozen deterministic predicates, not a learning self-judge that co-improves with generation.*

- **Initiative watch routes from fixed thresholds, not from a scored self-judgement.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/initiative_watch.py:583` `evaluate(report, entries)` is pure deterministic classification of `findings` with `id/kind/role/ref/summary/primary/escalation/action` and states `UNKNOWN/ATTENTION/OBSERVED` (line ~764 `_status`), driven by governing-variable checks (`GOVERNING_VARIABLES`, `MEADOWS_RANK`, `leverage_level`) and timeout windows (`AGED_SECONDS=3600`, `CONTINUATION_SECONDS=1800`, `ESCALATE_SECONDS=600`, `REMINDER_SECONDS=1800` — :76-82). The check in `check()` (:853) reads the tape, evaluates, persists `state.json`, and appends a DM via `Feed` — but the finding's `summary`/`action` text is templated from the rule, never scored by the plant judging its own prior summaries. There is no `pairwise accuracy` or `reward model` surface in this module; `rg -n "pairwise|exact.match|Spearman|Kendall|self.reward|LLM.as.a.Judge|AIFT" src/mishe_tauftauf/initiative_watch.py` is empty.

- **EFT seed discipline is absent — the plant has IFT-equivalent training but no judged seed filtering.** The closest analogue to SRLM's EFT filtering (keep only judgements whose score ranking agrees with human ranking, then rebalance) is `src/mishe_tauftauf/initiative.py:239` `_patches` + `follow_through.py` refusal projection, and `outcome_events.py:259` `classify_outcomes` — all deterministic parsing of wall/tape records, not a judge-score agreement filter. The plant does not store a `justification + 1..5 score` tuple per finding, nor average 3 sampled judgements, nor discard judgements that disagree with human-ranked ground truth.

- **No iterative AIFT loop — one report, one evaluation, no M1→M2→M3 DPO over self-scored preferences.** `initiative.py:297` `collect()` returns a single 24h window `{window, state, wakes, settlements, open_wakes, lifecycle_gaps, outcomes, ...}` and `initiative_watch.py:853` `check()` acts on it once per interval with file-locked `_archive`. SRLM's `Self-Instruction Creation → Instruction Following Training → Iterative Self-Alignment` (§2.2-2.4) has no counterpart: the plant does not generate new prompts from self, score them with self, build preference pairs, and retrain the scorer. The DGM archive (`initiative.py:48-61 INITIATIVE_ARCHIVE_VERSION/STAGED_SUBSETS/STAGED_TRIGGER/stepping_stone_score/is_stepping_stone`) added 2026-11-02 is the code-evolution loop; this cell's loop is the *judgement*-evolution loop.

- **Dual metric evaluation exists for delivery but not for judgement quality.** The plant measures `wakes`, `settlements`, `open_wakes`, `lifecycle_gaps`, `patches` counts, and `AlpacaEval`-style head-to-head is absent. SRLM's `§3.1.2 Reward Modeling` metrics — pairwise accuracy (pair order agreement), exact match (total order), Spearman, Kendall tau, fraction of 5/5 scores that are human-top-ranked — have no analogue in `initiative_watch.py`, which only reports `UNKNOWN` when evidence is missing/stale/future. A finding whose routing was correct but whose self-scored justification was poor is indistinguishable from a well-justified one.

*Consequence.* The loop is single-loop (Argyris 1976, as cited in `initiative_watch.py:149-196 GOVERNING_VARIABLES`): thresholds are given and findings repeat under the same variable without the double-loop candidate being named as judgement drift. `is_single_loop_trap` (:215) exists but has no reward-model accuracy signal to tell whether the trap is a scorer that stopped improving.

## What is new here

**Self-rewarding makes the plant's own judgement an improvable signal: the same watcher scores its own evidence quality (LLM-as-a-Judge prompting), preference pairs from those scores train the next judge, and judgement accuracy (pairwise/exact/Spearman/tau) co-improves with generation quality — iteratively, with seeded EFT filtering and averaged sampling.**

Five ideas transfer directly, each bounded by what Yuan et al. concede and each additive to the existing `OBSERVED/ATTENTION/UNKNOWN` and `BLOCKED/CLEAR` vocabulary.

1. **Joint EFT+IFT initialization as the typed seed discipline (SRLM §2.1, §3.2.1).** Today the plant has only deterministic IFT-equivalent initialization (parse tape, classify lifecycle). The transfer types `SELF_REWARDING_EFT_VERSION` and the predicate "judgement kept only when its score ranking agrees with human ranking" as the filter that makes a self-judge admissible at all. Limit kept: EFT does not replace deterministic checks — SRLM's `M1≈SFT` result shows adding judge training need not degrade the base capability, but only when filtered (rebalanced away from common score 4, p.6 footnote).

2. **LLM-as-a-Judge scoring with chain-of-thought + 1..5 rubric, sampled 3× averaged, as the bounded self-reward surface (§2.3 Figure 2 prompt, §3.1.3 sampled decoding T=0.6 p=0.9).** The plant's `evaluate()` would carry an optional `judgement: {score: 1..5, justification: str, prompt_version: str, samples: int}` beside each finding — a typed, bounded record (score 1..5, justification ≤4096 chars, samples=3 averaged), not an unbounded prose field. Limit kept: the rubric prompt matters (§3.2.2 ablation: prompt critical; footnote 2 notes the prompt mentions web search the model cannot do) — judgement quality is prompt-dependent, not model-inherent.

3. **Iterative self-alignment M1→M2→M3 as the typed iteration vocabulary (§2.4, §3.1.3 AIFT dataset sizes 3,964 → 6,942).** The transfer adds `SELF_REWARDING_ITERATION: Final[int]` and `AIFT_PAIR_COUNTS: Final[tuple[int,...]] = (3964, 6942)` as the *measurement* vocabulary for whether a finding's preference pairs came from `AIFT(M1)` vs `AIFT(M2)`, mirroring SRLM's `M1= EFT+IFT`, `M2= DPO_AIFT(M1)`, `M3= DPO_AIFT(M2)`. No training is performed here; the symbols only make the iteration attributable. Limit kept: prompt generation stays fixed-model Self-Instruct (Llama 2-Chat 70B, 6 IFT + 2 self-generated demos, ROUGE-L/keyword/length filtered) — not fully self-generated, and DPO is the explicit preference optimizer.

4. **Dual-metric evaluation: instruction following vs reward modeling co-evolution (§3.1.2, §3.2).** The transfer introduces pure reward-model metrics `pairwise_accuracy`, `exact_match`, `spearman`, `kendall_tau`, `top_score_is_human_best_rate` as the *judgement quality* surface beside the existing `wakes/settlements/patches` counts. A finding whose routing is `OBSERVED` but whose reward-model pairwise accuracy is low is now a measurable gap — exactly SRLM's separation of "did the model answer well" vs "did the model judge well." Limit kept: evaluation leans on external judges (GPT-4 head-to-head, Claude 2 validation) — the paper does not claim judge-free evaluation; the plant's detector likewise must name its evaluator.

5. **Ablation-aware guard: prompt and sampling matter, and NLP benchmarks must not regress (§3.2.1 last paragraph, §3.2.2 prompt importance).** The transfer makes `judge_prompt_version` and `sampling_params {T, p, samples}` typed fields so that a degraded judgement can be attributed to prompt/sampling change vs model change — and so that `MT-Bench`/`ARC/HellaSwag/GSM8K` style regression checks are a typed gap when a self-rewarding iteration improves win rate but loses NLP capability. Limit kept: SRLM found no NLP regression, but states the risk; the metric is the detector, not a guarantee.

**Limits the proposal keeps from the source.** No model weights are trained, no `Feed.append`/`seed yield`/`wall` mutation, no new lock or `WALL_MAX_*` change, no chat-protocol wire change. The vocabulary is pure predicates and bounded constants beside `evaluate()/check()`, import-safe without a site home, clock, or network. Frozen deterministic thresholds remain the source of truth; self-judgement is an *additional* scored overlay, not a replacement — the same way `M1≈SFT` preserved instruction following while adding judgement.
