## What we read

Contributor Roles Taxonomy — **CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy)**, community-owned taxonomy of 14 contributor roles, standardized as **ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022** — read authoritative specification at:

- <https://credit.niso.org/> (HTML, fetched 200 2026-11-02, 61,255 bytes, WordPress 7.1.3, canonical `https://credit.niso.org/`, title "CRediT – Contributor Role Taxonomy")
- <https://credit.niso.org/contributor-roles-defined/> (HTML, fetched 200 2026-11-02, 55,480 bytes, lists all 14 roles with definitions, link `https://credit.niso.org/wp-json/wp/v2/pages/46`)
- <https://credit.niso.org/contributor-roles/conceptualization/> (HTML, fetched 200 2026-11-02, 50,573 bytes, individual role page; pattern holds for 14 roles under `https://credit.niso.org/contributor-roles/<role>/`)
- NISO standard page via site badge: <https://credit.niso.org/> carries NISO logo (`NISO-LOGO-noTagline-RGB-72dpi.jpg`) and the taxonomy is identified across the site as ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022 (CRediT became NISO standard 2022; site is the maintained resource hub).

Origin paper (kept distinct, not the primary read): Brand, Holbrook, et al. — "Beyond authorship: attribution, contribution, collaboration, and credit", *Learned Publishing* 28(2):151–155, 2015, DOI `10.1087/20150211` — cited on the CRediT site as the foundational description; the site's 14-role list is the normative operational mechanism.

Six passages carry the operational mechanism, each with its stated limits kept.

1. **What CRediT is — 14 roles to complement authorship, not replace it (home + roles-defined intro).** Home: "CRediT is a community-owned taxonomy of 14 contributor roles designed to represent the key types of contributions typically made to research outputs, including journal articles. Originally developed to complement traditional authorship by capturing ..." Roles-defined intro lists the 14 roles as the closed vocabulary a publication can claim per contributor. Limit kept: CRediT does not define authorship order or corresponding-author duties — it complements the ICMJE four-criteria authorship the plant just typed, it does not supplant it.

2. **The 14 role definitions — verbatim closed vocabulary (contributor-roles-defined, 14 `<p>` blocks).** Each role is a term plus one-sentence definition:
   - `Conceptualization` – Ideas; formulation or evolution of overarching research goals and aims.
   - `Data curation` – Management activities to annotate (produce metadata), scrub data and maintain research data (including software code, where it is necessary for interpreting the data itself) for initial use and later re-use.
   - `Formal analysis` – Application of statistical, mathematical, computational, or other formal techniques to analyze or synthesize study data.
   - `Funding acquisition` – Acquisition of the financial support for the project leading to this publication.
   - `Investigation` – Conducting a research and investigation process, specifically performing the experiments, or data/evidence collection.
   - `Methodology` – Development or design of methodology; creation of models.
   - `Project administration` – Management and coordination responsibility for the research activity planning and execution.
   - `Resources` – Provision of study materials, reagents, materials, patients, laboratory samples, animals, instrumentation, computing resources, or other analysis tools.
   - `Software` – Programming, software development; designing computer programs; implementation of the computer code and supporting algorithms; testing of existing code components.
   - `Supervision` – Oversight and leadership responsibility for the research activity planning and execution, including mentorship external to the core team.
   - `Validation` – Verification, whether as a part of the activity or separate, of the overall replication/reproducibility of results/experiments and other research outputs.
   - `Visualization` – Preparation, creation and/or presentation of the published work, specifically visualization/data presentation.
   - `Writing – original draft` – Preparation, creation and/or presentation of the published work, specifically writing the initial draft (including substantive translation).
   - `Writing – review & editing` – Preparation, creation and/or presentation of the published work by those from the original research group, specifically critical review, commentary or revision – including pre- or post-publication stages.
   Limit kept: the 14 include three that ICMJE explicitly says alone do not qualify for authorship (`Funding acquisition`, `Resources`, `Project administration`/`Supervision` as general supervision) — CRediT types them as acknowledged contributions, not as authorship claims.

3. **Machine-readable binding — JATS XML and NISO standard (site footer/NISO badge + roles-defined structure).** The site presents CRediT as ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022 with NISO branding and per-role pages; the mechanism is designed to be embedded in JATS `<contrib>` with `<role>` vocabulary so that "who did what" is typed, not prose. Limit kept: the site does not mandate a degree (lead/equal/supporting) in the current 14-role display — degree was part of early CRediT but is not shown on the 2026-11-02 roles-defined page, so this transfer does not claim a degree vocabulary.

4. **Multiple roles per contributor, multiple contributors per role (roles-defined list semantics).** The list is a vocabulary, not a partition — one contributor can hold `Software + Validation + Writing – review & editing`, and one role can be held by many contributors. Limit kept: CRediT does not type effort magnitude or order — it types kind, not share.

5. **Not a publication check, not a truth claim (home description).** Home describes CRediT as capturing "the key types of contributions typically made" — a disclosure vocabulary, not a verdict that the contribution was correct. Limit kept: `Software` does not mean bug-free, `Validation` does not mean reproduced — same boundary as C2PA `SHOULD NOT provide value judgments` the plant already cites.

6. **Adoption as the honest-attribution mechanism (site adopters/how-to-implement).** The site lists `How to implement CRediT` and `Adopters of CRediT` — the mechanism's purpose is public, cross-publisher attribution that survives journal transfer because the vocabulary is shared. Limit kept: adoption is publisher-side disclosure at publication time, not post-hoc inference from commit logs.

**Why this literature, for this cell.** Field `publication-and-attribution` is "the reader book, the DEV blog, commits, CI and pushes" and angle is *mechanism* — an operational mechanism this area proposes that is implementable, not just philosophy. CRediT is the current (2015–present, NISO 2022) operational mechanism for "who did what" on a published artifact — exactly the honest-public-voice mechanism the plant needs where it currently writes `by <role>` or `Wall outcome <kind> by <role>` with a single string and no typed contribution.

## What this plant already does

*The plant attributes every publication by a single role string, with no typed distinction between kinds of contribution — so `Software`, `Funding acquisition`, and `Writing – review & editing` read identically.*

- **Wall and outcome authorship is a single `by <role>` header with ICMJE vocabulary proposed but not yet landed.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/wall.py:19-21` defines `WALL_MAX_BYTES/WALL_MAX_LINES`; `19-31` `outcome(home, role, kind, text, evidence)` formats `"Wall outcome <kind> by <role>"` and `_publish_outcome` enforces digest placement. The 2026-11-02 ICMJE revisit (`publication-and-attribution--misread--2026-11-02.md`, commit 3699d3c4) proposed `ICMJE_AUTHORSHIP_CRITERIA / CONTRIBUTORSHIP_STATUSES / ACKNOWLEDGMENT_ONLY_ACTIVITIES` but HEAD `wall.py` at 1311ee3b has not yet landed it (`rg -n "ICMJE|CREDIT|Conceptualization" src/mishe_tauftauf/wall.py` is empty). A wall note `by discover` and a wall note `by pub` carry the same single attribution regardless of whether the role supplied software, resources, or supervision.

- **Outcome publication types provenance, not contribution.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/outcome_publication.py:80-103` carries C2PA `DISCLOSURE_LEVELS / MANIFEST_STATES / PROVENANCE_ALG` plus Golaszewski `PROVENANCE_SECURITY_GOALS / REVOCATION_STATES` and helpers `provenance_data_hash / provenance_ingredient / provenance_validation_state` — binding and disclosure of bytes, not who conceptualized, investigated, or validated. `rg -n "Conceptualization|Data curation|Formal analysis|CRediT" src/` is empty plant-wide before this review.

- **CI/watch and delivery type Build-level provenance, not contributor kind.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/ci_watch.py:35-72` carries `SLSA_BUILD_LEVELS / SLSA_PROVENANCE_STATES / GITHUB_ATTESTATION_PREDICATES` with helpers `slsa_build_level_is_valid / gh_attestation_verify_args_is_valid` — who built the artifact and at what isolation level, not who funded, supervised, or wrote the review. A `Valid` outcome with `Software` contribution and a `Valid` outcome with `Funding acquisition` alone are indistinguishable by current vocabulary.

- **Checks type verdicts, not roles.** `src/mishe_tauftauf/post_check.py` types `MERTON_NORMS / SKEPTICISM_STATES` as a verdict lifecycle; `checks/` and `coord_tape.py` route addressed messages, but no symbol types the 14 contribution kinds a headed publication should disclose.

*Consequence the source predicts.* Without a shared contribution vocabulary, the plant's reader must infer "who did what" from the single `by <role>` string and from prose, so ghost contributions (writing assistance alone) and honorary attributions (supervision/funding alone) are invisible — exactly the misattribution CRediT was built to make observable, while keeping the ICMJE rule that disclosure is separate from authorship qualification.

## What is new here

**CRediT reframes the plant's `by <role>` from "who is credited" to "which of 14 typed kinds that contributor supplied, where `Funding acquisition / Resources / Supervision` alone remain acknowledged contributions under ICMJE and multiple roles per contributor are normal."**

Five ideas transfer directly, each bounded by what CRediT concedes.

1. **14 roles as the closed contribution vocabulary (roles-defined list).** Today the plant has `OUTCOME_KINDS` and `MERTON_NORMS` and (proposed) `ICMJE_AUTHORSHIP_CRITERIA`. The transfer adds `CREDIT_ROLES: Final = ("conceptualization","data-curation","formal-analysis","funding-acquisition","investigation","methodology","project-administration","resources","software","supervision","validation","visualization","writing-original-draft","writing-review-editing")` as the typed kind — `rg -n "conceptualization" src/` becomes observable rather than prose. Limit kept: no degree (lead/equal/supporting) is typed in this commit, matching the current site display.

2. **Complements ICMJE, does not compete with it — funding/supervision alone stay non-author (CRediT Funding acquisition / Resources / Supervision vs ICMJE II.A.3).** CRediT's own `Funding acquisition` and `Resources` are exactly the ICMJE "acquisition of funding; general supervision ... alone do not qualify" list. The transfer documents that a CRediT role can be held without being an ICMJE `author` — `Funding acquisition` → `acknowledged-contributor` under `authorship_criteria_met` is the visible boundary. Limit kept: the four-criteria conjunction still decides authorship; CRediT decides contribution kind.

3. **Multiple roles per contributor, multiple contributors per role (list semantics).** A single wall outcome `by field-review` that involved reading, formal analysis, and writing can be typed as `{formal-analysis, writing-original-draft}`, while `Software` can be jointly held by `genome` and `health`. Limit kept: kind, not magnitude or order — no percentage or author-order claim is typed.

4. **Machine-readable disclosure at publication time (ANSI/NISO Z39.104-2022 / JATS intent).** The plant's publication boundary can disclose `CREDIT_ROLES` alongside `ICMJE_AUTHORSHIP_CRITERIA` so a downstream reader or checker can parse `who did what` without scraping prose. Limit kept: the vocabulary is the detector — no wall write is blocked and no DEV article is corrected in this commit; undisclosed assistance is a typed gap, not a publication gate.

5. **Verdict-free, like C2PA — kind does not imply correctness (home description).** Holding `Software` does not claim the code is correct; holding `Validation` does not claim replication succeeded. Limit kept: the same `SHOULD NOT provide value judgments` boundary the plant already documents for C2PA applies — the role word names the contribution, the check decides the outcome.

**Limits the proposal keeps from the source.** No author-order change, no contribution-percentage, no degree qualifier, no JATS XML emission, no DEV front-matter write, no `Feed.append`/`seed yield`/`wall` mutation, no new lock or `WALL_MAX_*` change, no chat-protocol wire-format change, no X.509 signing or trust-list fetch — only the 14 kinds as an import-safe, pure vocabulary beside the wall's bounded prose, exactly the site's scope that a taxonomy, not a workflow, provides.
