## What is new here

The new piece from this source is a concrete metric second-order cybernetics actually uses to measure itself, and that this plant can compute without inventing `V_D`.

1. **Discrete stability under `±ε` as a count.** Von Foerster p.265 (i): a stable Eigen-Value absorbs any infinitesimal perturbation, an instable one jumps to another. For this plant, `AGREE` vs `DISAGREE` vs `UNKNOWN` is already discrete — a one-bit `checked_at` drift or a one-byte `check_sha256` change does not degrade gracefully; the reading jumps. The metric is literally countable: over the last N scheduled `observer-verdict-agreement` runs, how many `±ε` perturbations (one-field flips, one-bit hash changes, one-second `snapshot_age` steps) left the verdict unchanged (stable) vs moved it to another eigen (instable) vs left it `UNKNOWN` (zero eigen, Appendix A4). The plant already logs `elapsed`, `check_sha256`, and `snapshot_age` each run — the denominator is the run count, the numerator is the stability class. The previous Ashby metric needed an external `V_D`; this one needs only the verdict stream the plant already publishes.

2. **Composition homomorphism as a second count.** Eq.11 vs Eq.12 is an implemented homomorphism test: does the coordination of a composition equal the composition of coordinations? In plant terms, with `*` as field-set composition (`Obs1*Obs2` = union of two `VERDICT_FIELDS` subsets), Eq.11 (`COORD(Obs1*Obs2)=COORD(Obs1)*COORD(Obs2)`) holds iff the differences reported on the union equal the union of differences reported on the parts. A failure is Eq.12 — `COORD′(Obs1*Obs2)≠COORD′(Obs1)*COORD′(Obs2)` — "the whole is … different" (p.266). That is a second countable divergence: the fraction of field subsets where the `differences()` output is homomorphic. No new field is needed; the ten `VERDICT_FIELDS` supply the domain (p.263 n.1 — the observer chooses the domain).

3. **Zero eigen stays `UNKNOWN`.** Appendix A4 shows some coordinations have no eigenvalue — `UNKNOWN` stays `UNKNOWN`. This matches the plant's doctrine that missing/stale stays `UNKNOWN` rather than being forced to `AGREE/DISAGREE`, and warns against a meta-meta operator hierarchy to escape it (p.269, McCulloch 1945 heterarchy) — already cited in the companion misread review (2026-11-02, `src/mishe_tauftauf/wall_review.py`).

This complements, not repeats, the predecessor DISCARD: Ashby 1956 measured channel capacity `V_D−V_R` over an enumerated table; von Foerster 1976 measures whether the tokens the channel already emits are discrete eigen-values and whether their coordination is compositional — both countable from the same ten fields without enumerating disturbances.
