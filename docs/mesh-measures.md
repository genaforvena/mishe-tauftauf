# Mesh measures

[README](../README.md) · [Mesh](mesh.md)

The measurement side of the mesh: how stewardship concentration can be summarised, and the
frame behind the four coupling questions. Nothing here is needed to read the mesh or run the plant.

Concentration, if you measure it, is bound to a stated window. Shares of
stewardship — landings, locks, wall writes — can be summarised in several ways.
Entropy measures how hard it is to predict who acts. Gini measures inequality
across the chosen population, including participants with zero share. It is
zero when all shares are equal. The Nakamoto coefficient counts the fewest
participants who together hold more than half the total share. The
τ-decentralisation index uses a chosen threshold τ in place of half.
The Herfindahl–Hirschman index (HHI) sums squared fractional
shares and equals 1 when one participant holds everything. The window,
population, clustering rule and threshold shape the measurement. A lane can
gain participants while becoming more concentrated. That is the finding of
a systematization of the measures themselves — participation and
decentralisation come apart, and pre-processing choices can move the number
(Ovezik et al. 2025, [arXiv:2501.18279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18279)). So
the plant publishes no single concentration number. It publishes the checks it
can defend, and leaves the rest named and open.

## The four coupling questions

Each coupling is worth asking four questions of, because a coupling that
answers none of them is decoration. Can it still break and resume, or does
one fault end it? Can any mind join or use it without passing one hub? Does
it carry mixed origins without forcing one identity on them? And is its
declaration detachable — a map to be remade on each edit — or has it hardened
into a traced path that replicates without adapting? The questions come from a
wider published frame, the rhizome principles of Berkowitz and Bor (2026); the
plant keeps the questions rather than that vocabulary; the citation is at the
foot of this page. Its answers are its records and audits rather than a score:
no coupling here is scored, and each question is answered where the plant can
check it — the wall and its audit for a write, the tape and the outcome record
for a message, the branch and the CI run for a landing. A declared coupling
that no reading can check is not thereby true.

The rhizome frame is cited as Berkowitz and Bor 2026, [doi:10.24072/pcjournal.669](https://doi.org/10.24072/pcjournal.669).
